EAGLE FOOTBALL GROUP
Press release - Lyon, 18 June 2026

Transfer of Afonso Moreira to Bayer 04 Leverkusen

Eagle Football Group announces that Olympique Lyonnais has reached an agreement with
Bayer 04 Leverkusen for the transfer of Afonso Moreira. The transfer amount could reach
€33.6 million, including €4.1 million in add-ons. Olympique Lyonnais will also receive
10% of the capital gain on a future sale of the player.
